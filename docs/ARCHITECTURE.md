# Industrial AI Suite — Technical Architecture & Specifications

## 1. System Overview
Metrology & quality engineering platform for Aerospace & Automotive AS9102 Rev C FAI, ASME Y14.5 GD&T compliance, CMM DMIS 5.2 routines, and SPC process stability.

## 2. Modules
- **Computer Vision & Ingestion**: Headless OpenCV, PyMuPDF, TIFF multi-frame ingestion, contour circularity balloon extractor.
- **AS9102 Reporting**: Forms 1, 2, and 3 automated workbook generator.
- **Predictive Maintenance**: Isolation Forest anomaly scoring, Werner-Sothin spectral vibration feature extraction.
- **3D CAD MBD Engine**: 3D mesh ingestion, geometric topology, bounding extents, and WebGL visualization.
